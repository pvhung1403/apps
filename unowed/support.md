## Frequently asked questions

**Snowball or avalanche — which should I pick?**
**Snowball** pays the smallest balance first, so you clear whole debts sooner and stay motivated.
**Avalanche** pays the highest interest rate first, which usually costs the least interest overall.
Switch between them on the Plan tab and compare the debt-free date and "interest ahead" figures.

**Why is my balance or date different from my lender's statement?**
When you log a payment, Unowed first adds the interest it estimates since your balance was last exact
(your APR ÷ 365 for each day), so the balance stays close to your lender's. Plans assume interest
compounds monthly and each payment is made on time. Lenders may also charge fees or use promotional
rates. Use **Update balance from statement** on a debt to set the exact figure; estimates restart from
that date.

**I logged a payment by mistake — how do I fix it?**
Open the debt and look under **History**. Tap a payment to change its amount or date, or swipe left on
any entry to delete it. The balance is corrected automatically.

**It says my plan never finishes.**
Your monthly payment doesn't cover the interest on at least one debt. Raise the amount on the Plan tab
(or check the minimum payment and interest rate you entered).

**How do I add the widget?**
Long-press your Home Screen › tap **+** › search "Unowed" › choose **Debt-free countdown**.
Lock Screen versions are in the Lock Screen widget gallery. Widgets are part of Pro.

**My reminders don't arrive.**
Check iOS Settings › Notifications › Unowed. Reminders arrive two days before each debt's due day.

**Which currency does it use?**
The currency of your region in iOS Settings › General › Language & Region.

**How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?**
Settings › **Restore purchases**, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Unowed, or in the app: Settings › **Manage subscription**.
Lifetime is a one-time purchase and never renews.

**Does the app collect my data?**
No. See the [Privacy Policy](https://hungpv143.github.io/apps/unowed/privacy/).

**Can I get my data out?**
Yes, free: Settings › **Export debts & payments (CSV)**. Your history is never locked.
