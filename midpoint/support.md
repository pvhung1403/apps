## Frequently asked questions

**How do I play?**
Each round shows two cities. Tap the globe where you think the point exactly halfway between them is, then
tap **Lock in**. Halfway means along the shortest path over the Earth (the way planes fly), not across a
flat map. You score up to 1,000 points per round, 5,000 per puzzle.

**When does the new puzzle start?**
Every day at 00:00 UTC, and it's the same puzzle for everyone. The countdown on the home screen shows the
time left.

**How do streaks work?**
Finish the daily puzzle to extend your streak. Every 7 days played earns a streak freeze (you can hold up to
2), and a freeze covers one missed day automatically. Practice and the archive don't count toward streaks.

**What is Halfway Club?**
An optional subscription that unlocks the archive: every past daily puzzle, to play and improve your best
score. Today's puzzle, practice, streaks and levels are free.

**How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?**
Settings › **Restore Purchases**, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Halfway, or Halfway › Settings › **Manage Subscription**.

**The globe is blank or loads slowly.**
Map imagery comes from Apple Maps and needs an internet connection. Check your connection and try again.

**Can I turn off the sounds?**
Yes: Settings › **Sound effects**. The app also follows your iPhone's silent switch.

**My daily reminder doesn't arrive.**
Turn it on in Halfway › Settings › Daily reminder, and check iOS Settings › Notifications › Halfway. The
reminder is skipped on days you've already played.

**Does Halfway collect my data?**
No. See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/).
