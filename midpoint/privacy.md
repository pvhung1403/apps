# Halfway Privacy Policy

_Effective date: November 10, 2026_

Halfway: Daily Globe Game ("the app") is made by Hung Pham ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to
your information when you use the app. Short version: **we don't collect it.**

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Halfway has no account, no server of ours, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never
receive, see, sell or share your data.

## What stays on your device

- **Your game history and settings**: your answers and scores for each daily puzzle, best scores, streak, level,
  and your settings (sound, reminder time). They are stored on your iPhone in the app's private storage and
  included in your normal device backups, which Apple manages.
- **Reminders** are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. No push server is involved.

## Maps and place names (Apple services)

- The globe is drawn with **Apple Maps** (MapKit), which downloads map imagery from Apple.
- To name what lies at a puzzle's halfway point (for example "North Atlantic Ocean"), the app sends **the
  coordinates of that halfway point** to Apple's geocoding service. It never sends your location: the app doesn't
  ask for location permission and never reads where you are.
- Apple handles these requests under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions (Halfway Club) are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details. The
app checks your purchase status with Apple's StoreKit on your device.

## Children

Halfway is suitable for all ages and doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
