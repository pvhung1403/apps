# Unowed Privacy Policy

_Effective date: November 8, 2026_

Unowed: Debt Payoff Planner ("the app") is made by Hung Pham ("we", "us"). This policy explains
what happens to your information when you use the app. Short version: **we don't collect it.**

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The app has no account, no bank login, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no
tracking. We never receive, see, sell or share your data.

## What stays on your device

- **Your debts, payments and plan settings** (debt names, balances, interest rates, minimum
  payments, due days, payments you log, interest the app estimates between statements, your monthly
  budget and payoff strategy) are stored on your iPhone in the app's private storage.
- A **short summary of your plan** (debt-free date, total remaining, progress, the next debt to be
  paid off) is shared only with the app's own Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets on the same device.
- **Due-date reminders** are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. No push server is involved.
- Your data is included in your normal device backups, which Apple manages under your iCloud or
  computer backup settings.

## Exporting your data

Export is free and always available. If you use **Export (CSV)**, the app creates a file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes
next (Files, Mail, another app) is your choice; we never receive a copy.

## Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details. The app
checks your purchase status with Apple's StoreKit on your device.

## Not financial advice

The app calculates estimates from the numbers you enter. It does not connect to your bank or lender,
and its results are not financial, legal or tax advice.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

Delete a debt in the app to remove it and its payment history. Deleting the app removes all of its
data from your device.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
