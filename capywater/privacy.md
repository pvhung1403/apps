# CapyWater Privacy Policy

_Effective date: November 6, 2026_

CapyWater ("the app") is made by Hung Pham ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens
to your information when you use the app. Short version: **we don't collect it.**

## What we collect

**Nothing.** CapyWater has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
We never receive, see, sell or share your data.

## What stays on your device

- **Your hydration history and settings** (drinks you log, daily goal, units, chosen pet, reminder
  times) are stored on your iPhone in the app's private storage, shared only with CapyWater's own
  Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. They are included in your normal device backups, which Apple
  manages under your iCloud or computer backup settings.
- **Reminders** are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. No push server is involved.

## Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health:

- CapyWater **writes** each drink you log as Dietary Water (and Dietary Caffeine for coffee and tea).
- CapyWater **reads** Dietary Water logged by other apps to show your true daily total.
- This data is exchanged only between CapyWater and the Health app on your device. It is **never
  sent to us or any third party, never used for advertising or marketing, and never sold.**
- Health access is optional. You can turn it off in CapyWater (Settings › Apple Health) or in the
  Health app (Profile › Apps › CapyWater) at any time. The app keeps working without it.

## Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details. The app
checks your purchase status with Apple's StoreKit on your device.

## Children

CapyWater is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone,
including children.

## Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its data from your device. Data CapyWater wrote to Apple Health stays in
Health until you delete it there (Health › Browse › Nutrition › Water › Show All Data).

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
