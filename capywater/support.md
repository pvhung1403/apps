# CapyWater Support

Need help? Email **[pvhung1403@gmail.com](mailto:pvhung1403@gmail.com)** — please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I add the widget?**
Long-press your Home Screen › tap **+** › search "CapyWater" › choose **Onsen Tracker** (Small or Medium).
Tap the pour buttons on the widget to log water without opening the app.
Lock Screen widgets are listed as **Onsen Glance**.

**The Live Activity isn't showing.**
Open CapyWater › Settings › turn on **Live Activity & Dynamic Island**, and make sure Live Activities are
allowed in iOS Settings › CapyWater. The activity rests for the day once you reach your goal or swipe it away.

**My reminders don't arrive.**
Check iOS Settings › Notifications › CapyWater. With **Smart reminders** (Pro), CapyWater stays quiet
once your goal is met and while you're ahead of pace, so fewer reminders can be expected on good days.

**Can I use ounces?**
Yes: Settings › **Units** › Fluid ounces (oz). Your history converts automatically.

**Apple Health isn't syncing.**
Open the Health app › Profile › Apps › CapyWater and allow **Water** (and Caffeine). Then return to CapyWater.

**How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?**
Settings › **Restore Purchases**, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions › CapyWater, or CapyWater › Settings › **Manage Subscription**.
Lifetime is a one-time purchase and never renews.

**Does CapyWater collect my data?**
No. See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/).
