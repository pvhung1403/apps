# Halfway: Daily Globe Game

A daily geography game: every day, five pairs of cities. Spin the 3D globe and drop a pin where you think the
point exactly halfway between them is, measured along the globe the way planes fly. Watch the flight reveal the
answer, score up to 5,000 points, keep your streak and compare with friends. No ads, no account.
